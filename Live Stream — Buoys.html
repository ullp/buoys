Broadcast Directly From Our Studio Master Mix

This section maps out the best free or low-cost connection path using OBS, Radio Caster, Icecast, Radio Boost, and Mixcloud so we can take the studio master output live with minimal friction.

Recommended free chain for studio streaming

Best Connection Path

The cleanest approach is to take the master mix from your audio interface into OBS, send it through Radio Caster or OBS-compatible streaming output to an Icecast server, and optionally relay or promote the stream through Radio Boost while archiving or publishing long-form sessions on Mixcloud.

Step 1

Studio Master Out

Route the final stereo bus from your mixer or audio interface into the streaming computer as a clean master feed.

Step 2

OBS Studio

Use OBS to manage audio levels, add webcams or overlays, monitor latency, and prepare the outgoing stream.

Step 3

Icecast / Radio Caster

Send the encoded stream to a free Icecast-compatible endpoint. Radio Caster is a lightweight way to push the audio-only feed.

Step 4

Listeners + Archive

Share the live URL via Radio Boost and later upload polished recordings or full DJ/live sessions to Mixcloud.

  • Why this stack: it keeps the live signal under your control and does not depend on a single social network.
  • Best free core: OBS Studio + a free Icecast server is the most flexible foundation.
  • Audio-first fallback: if you do not need video, Radio Caster + Icecast is simpler than full OBS scene management.
  • Mixcloud role: best used for session publishing and discoverability, not as the main real-time studio transport layer.

Suggested Studio Workflow

How I’d Set This Up

1. Capture the master bus cleanly

Use the mixer’s record out, audio interface loopback, or a dedicated stereo pair so the stream hears the exact same master mix as the room monitors.

2. Normalize in OBS

Add the audio input capture, set a limiter to avoid clipping, and optionally add camera scenes, studio branding, and a “live now” slate.

3. Stream to Icecast

Use a free Icecast endpoint as the stable public stream destination. This gives you a simple URL that can be embedded later on the site.

4. Promote and archive

Push the listening link through Radio Boost and upload the best recorded sessions to Mixcloud for replay and audience growth.

Recommended Core

OBS Studio

The main technical hub for scenes, meters, audio control, webcams, and stream output.

  • Best all-round control for live studio streaming
  • Free and widely supported
  • Ideal if you want both audio and video

Recommended Transport

Icecast.com / Free Icecast Server

Best destination for a persistent, shareable stream URL when you want audio-first broadcasting.

  • Open streaming standard
  • Works with many encoders and players
  • Good base for embedding on the website later

Lightweight Encoder

Radio Caster

Useful if you want a simpler audio-only sender than OBS.

  • Fast to configure
  • Good for no-video studio sessions
  • Can feed Icecast directly

Distribution Support

Radio Boost

Helpful as a promotion or relay layer, but not the primary studio ingest point.

  • Use it to extend reach
  • Keep your main source on OBS + Icecast
  • Useful once the live URL is stable

Archive / Replay

Mixcloud

Great for publishing recordings and mixes after the live session finishes.

  • Best for discoverability and playback
  • Less ideal as the core low-latency live backbone
  • Strong companion to the main stream workflow
Bottom line: if the goal is “live stream directly from our music studio master mix” with free tools, I recommend OBS Studio → Icecast server as the main path, with Radio Caster as the simple audio-only alternative, Radio Boost for visibility, and Mixcloud for archived sessions.
